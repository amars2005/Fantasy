# EDA: prior-season stats vs next-season fantasy points

Generated by `scripts/eda_yoy.py` over 2015-2025. 3418 player-season pairs, prior season filtered to 8+ games, next season kept even when it is a zero.

All correlations are Spearman, computed within (season, position) and pooled by group size. Standard errors are across seasons, not players.
