## Is a lost season a warning about the player, or only his body?

Players whose prior season was cut short (under 10 games) against those who played through, matched on prior points per game. Left column is the effect on next-season *games*, right on next-season *points per game*.

| pos | effect on games | ± | effect on ppg | ± | n short |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| QB | **-2.12** | 0.37 | **-1.90** | 0.45 | 318 |
| RB | **-2.49** | 0.31 | **-0.79** | 0.23 | 599 |
| WR | **-2.27** | 0.24 | **-0.86** | 0.16 | 936 |
| TE | **-2.75** | 0.31 | **-0.81** | 0.16 | 498 |

The rate column has a confound. A short season measures points per game over few games, so `ppg_lag1` is a noisier control for exactly the players being tested, and a noisier control regresses them further toward the mean on its own. Matching on the season *before* the injury year instead uses a control the injury cannot have contaminated:

| pos | effect on ppg, clean control | ± | n short |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| QB | **-1.68** | 0.67 | 138 |
| RB | **-1.03** | 0.36 | 327 |
| WR | **-1.54** | 0.26 | 539 |
| TE | **-1.19** | 0.27 | 261 |
